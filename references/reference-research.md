# Successful-application research

Use successful cover letters and portfolios as evidence about evaluation patterns, never as text templates.

Source priority:

1. Employer recruiting guides, team blogs, hiring-manager talks, official employee interviews.
2. Identifiable public portfolios or detailed retrospectives from accepted candidates.
3. Linkareer, university career centers, credible communities with enough context.
4. Anonymous or SEO-oriented posts only as weak signals.

Prefer employer-, role-, level-, and time-matched examples. Record URL, publisher, date, access date, role context, verifiability, apparent rubric, repeated interview topics, problem-action-verification-impact framing, portfolio evidence density, transferable principles, confidence, and bias risks.

Never copy distinctive wording. A single accepted application is not a causal recipe. Verify every first-person statement against workspace evidence and separate team results from individual contribution.
