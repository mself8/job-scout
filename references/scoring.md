# Fit scoring

Score out of 100 using verified evidence.

| Dimension | Points |
|---|---:|
| Core requirements | 25 |
| Evidence strength and artifacts | 25 |
| Seniority and eligibility | 15 |
| Domain affinity | 10 |
| Portfolio reuse | 10 |
| Logistics | 10 |
| Product and culture signal | 5 |

A missing non-trainable hard requirement caps the score at 49. A missing trainable core stack caps it at 59 unless a working artifact closes the gap. Unknown deadline, eligibility, location, or availability prevents apply-now; mark verify-first. Distinguish required, preferred, and merely mentioned skills.

Bands: 80–100 strong apply and package; 70–79 apply after a small revision; 60–69 strategic stretch or proof project; below 60 archive or build-target.

Priority role clusters are candidate-specific. Read them from `$STATE_DIR/profile.md` and rank in the order listed there. Do not invent an ordering.

Do not rank a role highly merely because it is an internship. Every dimension requires direct evidence; a role outside every priority cluster needs a stronger artifact match, not a weaker one.

## Logistics penalties

Logistics is where candidate constraints recorded in `$STATE_DIR/preferences.md` are applied — working language, commute, start date, contract length. Apply these as deductions with the reason stated in the daily report, never as silent filters, and never as hard gates unless the posting itself states the requirement. A constraint the candidate has not confirmed is an unknown, not a penalty.
